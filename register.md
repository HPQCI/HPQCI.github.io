# Regitsration

For each accepted paper, **at least one author must register** for the Comptuing Frontiers 2026 conference by the registration date 06/04/2026, actively participate, and present the work.

To register for the workshop, got to [CF'26 registration page](https://www.computingfrontiers.org/2026/registration.html) and select the FULL AUTHOR REGISTRATION fee. This registration will give access to the full conference track. This registration type includes reception, breakfast, lunch, and breaks in the days of the conference events, plus the participation in the welcome reception on 19th May the social dinner on 20th May.

**Important Dates**
*	Registration deadline: April 6th, 2026 AoE

Registration site: [https://www.computingfrontiers.org/2026/registration.html](https://www.computingfrontiers.org/2026/registration.html)
